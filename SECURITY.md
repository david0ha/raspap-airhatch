# Security policy

Report vulnerabilities in airhatchd, the plugin, the system files or the
layer privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting:
<https://github.com/david0ha/raspap-airhatch/security/advisories/new>.
Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

The full policy, including what belongs to RaspAP or meta-raspap instead and
what not to attach to a report, is in
[meta-raspap-airhatch/SECURITY.md](meta-raspap-airhatch/SECURITY.md).
